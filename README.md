# SENTINEL v4.2 — A Surveillance State Terminal Game

> *"If you want a picture of the future, imagine a boot stamping on a human face — forever."*
> — George Orwell, 1984

---

## About

**SENTINEL** is a terminal-based narrative game where you play as an anonymous Government Data Analyst seated at a secure computer terminal. Your job is to monitor citizens and neutralize threats.

Over three levels — drawn from dystopian literature, real historical events, and present-day surveillance controversies — you are handed six dossiers and forced to make a choice: enforce the system, or protect the individual.

The game argues that when humanity grants authorities unrestricted visibility into private life, power inevitably corrupts — transforming citizens into targets and reducing human dignity to data.

Inspired by *Papers, Please* (Lucas Pope, 2013).

## How to Play

**Requirements:** Python 3.6 or higher. No external libraries needed.

```bash
python main.py
```

At the login screen, enter any Analyst ID and passphrase. Your choices across all six scenarios determine your ending.

**Tip:** Pressing Enter at any point skips the scrolling text.

---

## Endings

Your compliance score (0–6) determines the outcome:

- **≥ 4 surveillance choices** — The system commends you as a Model Analyst.
- **≤ 3 surveillance choices** — A Security Breach alarm triggers and your terminal is locked.

Both endings are designed to be disturbing.
